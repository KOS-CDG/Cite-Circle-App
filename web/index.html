Your Information & Permissions

Sandboxed Local Storage

Cite Circle stores papers, reading vault entries, and discussion cache inside isolated browser storage. No unauthorized file access or unrequested tracking cookies are permitted.

Privacy Policy & Sandboxing

1. Academic Data Sovereignty

Cite Circle is engineered with an offline-first architecture. All research manuscripts and discussions are parsed in sandboxed memory without third-party ad pixels or tracking.

2. Cloud Authentication

Authentication is conducted directly with Google Firebase Auth (Project: cite-circle-3857b) with strict HTTPS transport and encrypted identity tokens.

3. File Security Guard

Preprint upload rejects ZIP, RAR, EXE, and executable scripts to defend scholarly networks against malicious payloads.

Terms of Service & Ethics

Academic Integrity & Fair Attribution

Authors retain full copyright of submitted manuscripts and preprints. Attribution follows standard DOI and BibTeX provenance rules.

Open Peer Review

Scholarly discourse is encouraged to remain constructive, empirical, and compliant with international scientific norms.

Account Ownership & Control

In compliance with GDPR Right to Erasure, you have complete sovereignty over your academic identity, downloaded cache, and credentials.
